About OCG Marketplace

OCG Marketplace is an independent online store offering trading cards, coins, custom slabs, art, ceramics, vintage collectibles, home goods, food items and other unique merchandise.

We focus on clear listings, fair pricing, secure payments and careful packing.

Shipping Policy

  • Orders may ship with USPS or UPS depending on package size, weight and destination.
  • Shipping charges are shown separately on the product or during checkout.
  • Most orders are prepared within 1–3 business days.
  • Tracking is provided when the selected shipping service includes tracking.
  • Large, fragile or heavy items may have higher shipping charges.
  • Please make sure your shipping address is correct before paying.

Return & Refund Policy

All sales are final. We do not accept returns or exchanges for buyer’s remorse, change of mind, or ordering the wrong item.

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or significantly different from the listing description, please contact OCG as soon as possible after delivery. We will review the issue and, when appropriate, provide a replacement, partial refund, or full refund.

Please keep the original item, shipping box and packing materials. Clear photos of the item and packaging may be required before a claim can be reviewed.

Any approved refund will be sent back through the original payment method. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the problem was caused by OCG or the carrier and OCG approves the refund.

Payment & Order Policy

  • Payments are processed through PayPal on supported listings.
  • Applicable sales tax is added at checkout where required.
  • Orders are not considered confirmed until payment is successfully completed.
  • OCG may cancel and refund an order if an item becomes unavailable or a listing contains a material pricing or inventory error.

Privacy Policy

OCG Marketplace does not sell customer personal information. Information provided for an order is used only to process payment, fulfill shipping, communicate about the order, prevent fraud and provide customer support.

Payment information is handled by the payment provider used at checkout. OCG does not directly store full payment-card details on this website.

Terms & Collectibles Disclaimer

Product photos and descriptions are provided to represent each item as accurately as possible. Colors and appearance may vary slightly due to lighting, photography and display settings.

OCG is an independent marketplace and custom slab brand. Unless a listing specifically states otherwise, an OCG custom slab is not a third-party certification or grade from PSA, SGC, BGS or another independent grading company.

Customers are responsible for reviewing the complete listing before purchase.

Food & Beverage Policy

Food and beverage products may be subject to different tax rates and additional state or local rules. Tax treatment depends on the exact product type. Regulated, restricted, perishable, homemade or age-restricted items may not be offered unless all applicable requirements are met.

Contact & Support

For order questions, shipping issues, damaged-item claims or product questions, use the contact method provided with your order confirmation or the official OCG Marketplace contact channel.

Please include your order information and a short description of the issue so we can help quickly.
